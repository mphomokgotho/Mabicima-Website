ABOUT MABICIMA

Who We Are

I am Gracious from Butswana Village. MABICIMA started in 2020 with one hen.

We are not just resellers. We mix our own maggot feed for free range chickens using our village formula of maggot protein, maize and moringa.

We mix feed, grow chickens, slaughter and train. We grow the market from Butswana to Maruleng.

OUR SERVICES

What We Do

From feed production to poultry farming and training.

01

We Mix Feed

We formulate and mix affordable, protein-rich feed for free range, Boschveld, broilers and layers.

Starter, grower and layer mixes are available.

02

Live Chickens

We grow and sell Free Range, Boschveld, Broilers and Egg Layers.

From day-old chickens to point-of-lay.

03

Slaughtered Chickens

Fresh slaughtered chickens are available every Friday and Saturday.

Whole or pieces, cleaned and packed. Bulk orders are available.

04

Breeding Training

Learn how we mix feed, breed chickens, slaughter and sell poultry products.

Training includes a certificate.
