WHO WE ARE

About MABICIMA

MABICIMA was started by Gracious from Butswana Village in Maruleng. The journey began in 2020 with one hen and a vision to build a sustainable poultry business.

Today, MABICIMA develops its own poultry feed formula using maggot protein, maize and moringa.

We mix our own feed, grow chickens, slaughter chickens and provide breeding training while working to grow the poultry market from Butswana to Maruleng.
